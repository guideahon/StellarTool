Version 0.6.6

Fixed: the Builder could turn a scripted encounter into a mini-boss and soft-lock
you. Some fights are staged by the game: an arena closes behind you and the
barrier only drops when the enemy dies. Those spawns were being converted like
any other, and a mini-boss carries the boss actor type, which the game only
honours for enemies it registers as bosses. The result was an enemy with no
health bar, impossible to target, stuck out of combat, inside a barrier that
would never open. The Builder now leaves those encounters vanilla: any spawn
that fires events on death, on battle or on spawn, that has trigger conditions,
that runs on MetaAI, or that gates a zone transition is skipped. On a full
allRegions build that is 508 spawns kept as-is, next to the 2 already skipped
for the respawn farm. Ambient enemies keep converting exactly as before, so the
mini-boss density you pick still means the same thing. Reported by FengYeLy.

If you are already stuck inside one of those barriers, install this build and
reload your save: the enemy alias is not stored in the save file, it is resolved
from the mod's tables every time the zone loads, so the fight comes back as a
normal enemy you can kill.

New: "Build ZIP only", right under "Build my mod". Same build, but it never
touches the game folder: no copy into ~mods, no edit of mods.txt. It just leaves
the ZIP in your output folder. Handy for building a mod for someone else, or for
looking at a build before you install it.

Everything from 0.6.5 is included.
