Version 0.3.13

- Your choices survive a re-analysis. Adding or removing a mod forced a fresh
  analysis, and that wiped every unticked change, every hand-edited value and
  every conflict you had already resolved. They are now carried over. If the
  mod that won a conflict is gone, that conflict goes back to unresolved
  instead of pointing at nothing.
- The mod list can now show which tables each mod actually contains. The
  changes list only shows tables that differ from vanilla, so there was no way
  to see the full contents of a pak. Click "Tables" on a mod to list them.

Reminder, since it is easy to miss: the Conflicts page has a button per mod to
give that mod every conflict at once, instead of resolving them one by one.
